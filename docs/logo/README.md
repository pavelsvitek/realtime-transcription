# Amie logo (peach navy palette)

Two locked versions of the Amie app icon, drawn on a 512 x 512 canvas.

| File | Background |
| --- | --- |
| [amie-logo-gradient.svg](amie-logo-gradient.svg) | Slight diagonal gradient, `#1D5880` to `#0D324D` |
| [amie-logo-solid.svg](amie-logo-solid.svg) | Flat `#0D324D` |

Both SVGs are generated by [build-logo.mjs](build-logo.mjs). Do not hand-edit the SVGs. Change the script, run `node docs/logo/build-logo.mjs`, and commit the output.

The design keeps the shape of the previous pink-orange icon (`apps/amie/src-tauri/icons/icon.png`): a white "A" with a waveform cut into its crossbar. Only the colors changed.

Source designs live in the Paper file "Amie Website", page "Palette · Peach navy" (artboards "Logo — Peach navy · Gradient" and "Logo — Peach navy · Solid"). The SVGs here are the source of truth.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy (primary) | `#0D324D` | Solid background, and the gradient end (bottom right) |
| Gradient start | `#1D5880` | Top left. A lighter navy, same hue, so the shift is slight |
| A and waveform | `#FFFFFF` | Pure white, no tint |
| A shadow | `#04141F` at 28% opacity | A very dark navy, not black |

No accent color is used in the logo. Peach (`#F2A07B`) belongs to the UI, not the icon.

## Shapes, back to front

### 1. Container: squircle

- A superellipse, `|x|^n + |y|^n = r^n`, with exponent `n = 4`.
- Radius 256, centered at (256, 256), so it fills the whole canvas edge to edge.
- Sampled at 120 points and joined with straight segments.
- Fill: the gradient or the solid navy.
- Gradient: linear, from the top left (0, 0) to the bottom right (1, 1) of the shape's bounding box. Stop 0 is `#1D5880`, stop 1 is `#0D324D`.

### 2. The "A"

One white even-odd path made of two sub-paths, with a 12 px stroke in the same white and `stroke-linejoin: round`. The stroke rounds the corners and grows the outline by 6 px on each side. The hole coordinates already allow for this.

1. Outline, with the notch between the feet:
   `M222 126 L292 126 L390 374 L328 374 L304 318 L210 318 L186 374 L124 374 Z`
   - The top is flat, 70 wide (x 222 to 292), centered on x = 257.
   - The outer legs run straight down to the feet at x 124 and 390, both at y = 374.
   - The feet are 62 wide (x 124 to 186 and 328 to 390).
   - The gap under the crossbar has its roof at y = 318, from x 210 to 304.
2. One combined hole: the counter triangle opening straight into the crossbar pill, cut out so the background shows through:
   `M257 182 L293 256 L305 256 A26 26 0 0 1 305 308 L203 308 A26 26 0 0 1 203 256 L221 256 Z`
   - The triangle tip is at (257, 182). Its sides run down to the pill's top edge at (221, 256) and (293, 256).
   - The pill is 102 wide plus the caps, 52 tall (y 256 to 308), with fully round ends (radius 26).
   - After the stroke shrinks it, the visible pill is about 148 x 40 at x 183 to 331, y 262 to 302.
   - There is no solid bar between the triangle and the pill. An earlier version had a separate triangle and pill with a band between them. That band read as a thick horizontal line above the waveform at small sizes, so the two are now one shape.

### 3. Shadow under the A

- The same A path, drawn first, shifted down by 9 px (`translate(0 9)`).
- Fill and stroke `#04141F`, both at 28% opacity, same 12 px round stroke.
- No blur. It is a hard-edged offset copy, because Paper's renderer dropped a blurred filter. It reads as a soft ledge under the A.

### 4. Waveform in the crossbar

- 21 white vertical bars, each 3 px wide with fully round ends (`rx = 1.5`).
- Bar `i` (0 to 20) has its left edge at `x = 192 + i * 6.3`, so the pitch is 6.3 px. The bars span x 192 to about 321.
- All bars are vertically centered on y = 282, the middle of the crossbar. A bar of height `h` starts at `y = 282 - h / 2`.
- Heights, left to right, symmetric around the middle bar:

```
8  12  18  14  22  18  28  22  34  26  36  26  34  22  28  18  22  14  18  12  8
```

- The pattern is a swell with small dips (a rise-and-dip every other bar) and peaks at 36 in the middle. The tallest bar leaves 2 px of space above and below it inside the pill.

## Order of drawing

1. Squircle
2. A shadow
3. White A
4. Waveform bars

## Sizing notes

- At 32 to 40 px the waveform shrinks to a faint stripe, and the A and counter still read clearly.
- The generated SVGs do not include a margin. For platform icons that need padding (for example macOS), scale the whole drawing and keep the squircle as is.
